Hi, I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Civil and Systems Engineering at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), advised by Professor [Yury Dvorkin](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/uzi-yury-dvorkin/). I am also affiliated with the [Ralph O'Connor Sustainable Energy Institute](https://energyinstitute.jhu.edu/), the [Critical Minerals Research Lab](https://www.rff.org/topics/transportation/critical-minerals/critical-minerals-research-lab/), the [Center for Systems Science and Engineering](https://systems.jhu.edu/), [Electric Power Innovation for a Carbon-free Society](https://energyinstitute.jhu.edu/epics/), and the [Academic Center for Reliability and Resilience of Offshore Wind](https://arrowosw.org/).

My research is interdisciplinary and focuses on how power systems evolve under physical, operational, market, and policy constraints. I study power system planning, electricity and capacity markets, reliability, and resilience, with particular attention to critical mineral and grid-equipment supply chains, emerging large-scale loads such as AI data centers, and policies shaping the energy transition. My work aims to understand how these factors affect investment, system operations, resource adequacy, and the reliability and pace of power system transformation.

I received my M.Sc. in 2022 from the Department of Environmental Health and Engineering at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), where I was advised by Professor [Ben Hobbs](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/benjamin-hobbs/). I received my B.E. in 2020 from the School of Environmental Science and Engineering at [Tianjin University](https://en.tju.edu.cn/). During my senior year, I completed an exchange program and my undergraduate thesis in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the [University of California, Irvine](https://uci.edu/).

Before beginning my Ph.D., I was a Research Associate (2022–2023) at the [21st Century China Center](https://china.ucsd.edu/) in the School of Global Policy and Strategy at the [University of California, San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/), where I worked with Professor [Michael Davidson](https://gps.ucsd.edu/faculty-directory/michael-davidson.html). I also worked as a Consultant at [Tsintergy](https://www.tsintergy.com/) in 2021.
