I am a strong believer that sometimes the best way to solve a problem is to close the laptop and do something completely unrelated. Outside research, I enjoy road trips, sports, cars, and music. I have played badminton and soccer since childhood and represented my schools in competitions at different stages of my education. I have followed Manchester United since 2010 and started bouldering in 2025. I also enjoy working on and modifying cars, playing piano and drums, and experimenting with digital music production using Vocaloid and Synthesizer V.

### 🚗 Road Trips

Road trips are one of our favorite ways to take a break from research and explore new places. Most of our trips are around the East Coast of the United States and usually combine scenic drives, hiking, small towns, beaches, and national parks.

Our frequent travel companions are **Jiu Jiu**, our black cat, and **Tu Tu**, a Golden Retriever who belongs to a close friend and often joins us on longer trips.

Jiu Jiu is leash-trained and surprisingly comfortable traveling and hiking. She has joined us on many trips and has gradually become an experienced road-trip companion.
